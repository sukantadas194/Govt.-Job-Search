SELECTION PROCESS:
6.1 Only those candidates fulfilling the eligibility criteria mentioned at point 3 and have registered their
application on the portal of NIC shall be considered for further selection process in NIC.
6.2 Eligible candidates will be shortlisted for interview/interaction, on the basis of GATE Score in the
ratio of 1:5 subject to their availability in order of discipline-wise and category-wise merit list. If there
are more than one candidate with the same GATE Score (while shortlisting for interaction/interview),
then all the candidates with the same Score will be shortlisted and called for interaction/interview
irrespective of the ratio stated above.
6.3 Equal weightage will be given to GATE 2024, GATE 2025 and GATE 2026 Scores.
6.4 Shortlisted candidates can download their call letters for the Personal Interaction/Interview from the
link available on the website (www.recruitment.nic.in). Candidates will have to regularly visit NIC
recruitment website for link or any other information.
6.5 Mere fulfilling of requirements as laid down in the advertisement does not qualify a candidate to be
called for personal interaction/interview.
6.6 Document verification will be conducted before Interaction/Interview. In case a candidate does not
appear for document Verification along with original documents, he/she shall not be eligible for
personal interaction/interview/provisional selection, and his/her candidature shall be rejected.
6.7 Upon completion of document Verification, candidates found eligible only shall be allowed to appear
for interaction/interview, subject to fulfillment of all other eligibility criteria prescribed for the post.
6.8 Candidates will be assessed in the interaction/interview based on the following attributes (Max marks
50):
a) Technical (Academic) knowledge
b) Presentation/Communication Skill/Comprehension
c) Academic proformance (0.05 mark for each percentage in the degree of Essential qualification)
In case of CGPA, if the university does not provide a formula, the candidate must provide a
certificate from the Registrar to that effect.
6.9 Only those candidates securing minimum qualifying marks of 50% (for Unreserved category) and 40%
(for EWS/OBC/SC/ST/PwBD category) in the Personal Interaction/Interview will be considered for
preparation of the final merit list. Candidates who do not qualify the interaction/interview will not be
considered for final selection irrespective of their GATE score.
6.10 The final selection of the candidates will be purely on the basis of category-wise and discipline-wise
merit of aggregate of 60% weightage of gate score and 40% weightage of marks in personal
interview/interaction.

6.11
Resolution of TIE cases:
In case of a tie in the total marks (GATE Score weightage + Personal Interaction/Interview weightage)
scored between two or more candidates, following criteria will be adopted in order of sequence for
deciding position in merit list:
First - The candidate with higher scores in the GATE paper shall be ranked higher in the merit list.
Second - If two candidates have equal scores in the GATE paper, then candidate with more marks in
the Technical (Academic) knowledge section of the interaction/interview, would be ranked higher.
Third - If there is still a tie, then candidate older in age shall be ranked higher in the merit list.
