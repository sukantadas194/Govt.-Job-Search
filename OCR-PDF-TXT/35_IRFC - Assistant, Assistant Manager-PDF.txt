Indian     Railway     Finance     Corporation        Limited     ( IRFC ),     is a schedule     ' A ',   Navratna     Public     Sector
Undertaking        under   the   Administrative        control     of Ministry     of Railways .     The   Company     is a Public
Finance     Institution     engaged     in supporting        creation     of infrastructure        in Railway     Sector     in India .   Its
main   business     activity     has   been   leasing     of Rolling     Stocks     ( Locomotives ,        Passenger     Coaches     and
Freight     Wagons )     and   Railway     projects     to Indian     Railways .     The   Company     raises   financial     resources
from   diverse     financial     markets     using   a variety     of instruments        to fund   acquisition     of these   assets .   IRFC
has consistently        been   rated   as EXCELLENT        by Govt .   of India   in achievements        of its MOU   targets .
As on March     31t ,   2025   the   turnover     of the   Company     is ? 27,152.14     crore   and   net   worth     is ?
52,667.77     crore .
