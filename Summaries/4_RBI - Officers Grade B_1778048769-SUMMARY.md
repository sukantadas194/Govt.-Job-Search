✅ **Eligibility Criteria**
**Educational Qualification:**
**For Grade 'B' (DR) - General:**
- Bachelor's Degree in any discipline with a minimum of 60% marks or equivalent grade (50% for SC/ST/PwBD if posts are reserved for them) OR Post-Graduation in any discipline with a minimum of 55% marks or equivalent grade (pass marks for SC/ST/PwBD if posts are reserved for them).
**For Grade 'B' (DR) - DEPR:**
- MA / MSc in Economics or equivalent from a recognized University/Institute with minimum 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/PwBD if posts are reserved for them). Economics must be the principal subject.
**For Grade 'B' (DR) - DSIM:**
- Master's Degree with minimum 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/PwBD if posts are reserved for them) in Statistics, Mathematics, Mathematical Statistics, Applied Statistics, Quantitative Economics, Econometrics, Informatics, or equivalent.
- OR Four-year Bachelor's Degree with minimum 60% marks (50% for SC/ST/PwBD if posts are reserved for them) in Statistics, Mathematical Statistics, Applied Statistics, Economics, Econometrics, Quantitative Informatics, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, or equivalent.
