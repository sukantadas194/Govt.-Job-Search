### Assistant (NE5 Level) / Assistant Manager (Finance) (E1 Level)

💼 **Compensation & Benefits**
- **Salary Structure:**
    * Assistant (NE5 Level): ₹21,000 - ₹74,000
    * Assistant Manager / Finance (E1 Level): ₹40,000 - ₹1,40,000
- **Gross Emoluments:** Approx. ₹6.5 Lakh per month (Assistant), Approx. ₹1.33 Lakh per month (Assistant Manager/Finance)

✅ **Eligibility Criteria**
**Educational Qualification:**
- **Assistant:** Three years Bachelor's Degree in Commerce from a recognized University/Institute with not less than 60% marks; OR Intermediate Examination Passed from the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India / Institute of Cost Accountants of India.
- **Assistant Manager / Finance:**
    * Three years Bachelor's Degree in Commerce from a recognized University/Institute with not less than 60% marks; AND Qualified CA/CMA from Institute of Chartered Accountants of India / Institute of Cost Accountants of India.
    * OR Two years Regular MBA (Finance) / Post Graduation Diploma in Management (Finance) from a recognized University/Institute.
- Candidates must possess essential qualification as on the closing date of online registration. Appearing candidates/candidates awaiting final results are NOT eligible.

**Age Limit:**
- **Minimum Age:** 18 years
- **Maximum Age:** 30 years
- *Relaxation in upper age limit for SC/ST/OBC candidates as per government norms.*
Age will be reckoned as on: 01.01.2026.

>
> **Selection Process:**
>
> Computer Based Test (CBT)
>           ↓

>
> **Exam Pattern & Syllabus**
>
> **Computer Based Test (CBT)**
> * **Total Marks:** 100
> * **Total Time:** 90 minutes (120 minutes for eligible PwD candidates with Scribe)
> * **Negative Marking:** 0.25 marks per wrong answer
> * **Subjects:**
>   * **Part A:**
>     * Mathematics/Numerical Ability: 20 Marks, 20 Questions, [Minutes] Min
>     * General Awareness: 10 Marks, 10 Questions, [Minutes] Min
>     * Logical Reasoning/General Intelligence: 10 Marks, 10 Questions, [Minutes] Min
>     * Knowledge about Railways/IRFC: 10 Marks, 10 Questions, [Minutes] Min
>   * **Part B:**
>     * **Assistant (Post Code 01):** Corporate Accounting, Income Tax Laws and Practice, Indirect Tax Laws, Corporate Laws, Cost Accounting, Computer Application in Business, Management Principles and Applications, Fundamentals of Financial Management, Auditing and Corporate Governance, Financial Reporting and Analysis, Project Management, E-Filing of Returns, Banking and Insurance, etc. (50 Marks, 50 Questions, [Minutes] Min)
>     * **Assistant Manager (Finance) (Post Code 02):** Accounting and IND AS Standards, Financial Reporting, Strategic Financial Management, Auditing, Professional Ethics, Cost and Management Accounting, Corporate & Economic Laws, Direct Tax Laws, Indirect Tax Laws, Enterprise Information Systems, Risk Management, Financial Services & Capital Markets, Computer Application in Business, E Filing of Returns, etc. (50 Marks, 50 Questions, [Minutes] Min)
>
> **Interview (for Assistant Manager / Finance - Post Code - 02)**
> * **Total Marks:** 20 (Calculated as 20% weightage of total marks)
>
> **Final Merit:**
> Final selection will be based on the candidate's performance in all stages of the selection process. Merit list will be prepared based on the total marks obtained by candidates in the Written Exam and Interview (for Assistant Manager (Finance)) after applying applicable reservation norms and cut-offs. For Assistant (NE5 Level), final merit will be based on CBT marks only.
